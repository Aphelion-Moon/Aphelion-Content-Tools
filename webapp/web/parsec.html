Meet the mascot

About Parsec

Parsec is this app's resident husky -- she lives in the sidebar on every page, sits or trots depending on whether a background job is running, and delivers a speech-bubble whenever something worth noticing happens: a save, a commit, an error, a completed refresh. Pat her any time; she doesn't mind.

Artwork: cropped and adapted from "Husky Sprites" (OpenGameArt.org), released CC0 / public domain. No attribution was required, but credit is given anyway.

Known Windows gotcha: Windows 10's Settings > Ease of Access > Display > Show animations in Windows toggle (confirmed against a real machine) maps straight to the browser's "reduce motion" preference. With it off, Parsec used to freeze -- she'd still switch which pose she showed, but never actually cycle frames or pace her box. She now animates by default regardless of that setting (see Motion below), but if she ever looks static again on a machine where you haven't touched her own Motion setting, check that Windows toggle first.

Accessibility

Motion

By default Parsec always animates -- she's a small sprite fully confined to her own box, not the large-scale motion your system's reduced-motion setting is meant to guard against, so it's ignored by default. Choose "Follow system setting" here if you'd rather she respected it, or "Always reduce motion" to keep her still no matter what.
